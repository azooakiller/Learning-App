Lesson 14: Color Theory in Practice

⏱ 23:40 📅 Dec 2024 👨‍🏫 James Wright
Overview
My Notes
Q&A

In this lesson you'll learn the foundations of color theory as applied to modern UI design. We cover the color wheel, complementary and analogous palettes, contrast ratios for accessibility (WCAG AA/AAA), and practical techniques for building a cohesive design system palette.

What you'll learn
✓ The 60-30-10 color rule
✓ HSL vs HSB color spaces
✓ WCAG contrast ratios
✓ Building a token system

How do I get my certificate?▾
Complete all lessons and pass the final quiz (score ≥ 70%). Your certificate will be automatically issued and available under My Progress → Certificates.
